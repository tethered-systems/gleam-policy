Gleam Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Gleam (package com.gleam, "the app") handles, published by tethered.systems ("we", "us"). In short: we don't collect any personal information ourselves. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to do that, as described below.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and the app has no analytics of its own.

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, scores, Sparks, streaks and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads (a banner on the main menu and occasional full-screen ads between levels) using the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google LLC. To show, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process the following information from your device:

InformationUsed for
Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set IDShowing and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention
IP address, used to estimate your approximate location (for example, your country or city)Showing relevant ads, complying with local laws, fraud prevention
Interactions with ads and the app (for example, app launches, ad views and taps)Ad delivery, measurement and analytics
Diagnostic information (for example, crash and performance data from the ads SDK)Keeping the ads service working and secure

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

3. In-app purchase

You can buy "Remove ads", a one-time purchase that turns off ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your name, payment card or billing address. Google Play tells the app only whether your Google account owns the purchase, and the app saves that on your device. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Your choices

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a region with similar laws, the app asks for your consent (using Google's consent form) before ads use your data for personalisation. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the app.
  • Personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's settings (on most devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling the app removes all game data from your device. We hold no data about you to delete. For data held by Google for advertising, use the controls above or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com.

5. Permissions

The app requests internet and network-state access, used to load ads and to reach Google Play for the purchase, the advertising ID permission, which the ads SDK uses as described above, and Google Play's billing permission for the purchase. It does not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or any other personal data.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

7. Security and retention

Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We keep no data of our own. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
